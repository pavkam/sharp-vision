## Callouts

> [!NOTE] Note
>
> Useful context that readers should notice while scanning.

---

> [!TIP] Tip
>
> Helpful advice for completing a task more effectively.

---

> [!IMPORTANT] Important
>
> Essential information required to complete the current goal.

---

> [!WARNING] Warning
>
> Urgent **formatted** information with `code` and a
> [reference](https://example.com/warning).

---

> [!CAUTION] Caution
>
> A risky action may have negative or irreversible consequences.
